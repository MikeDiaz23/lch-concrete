Residential & Commercial

Talk about your project

From a family’s front walk to a business entrance or a city sidewalk, every project starts with understanding the property and the people it serves. LCH Concrete & Restoration helps homeowners, businesses, and public property teams define their concrete needs and plan practical next steps south of Atlanta, GA, and in surrounding cities.

Homeowners

A driveway, patio, or walkway should fit the way you use your home. Whether you are replacing damaged concrete, adding room outdoors, or improving the connection between spaces, we start with your priorities and the condition of the property.

  • Discuss where repairs may be appropriate and where replacement or a new pour may make more sense.
  • Plan dimensions, surface finishes, drainage considerations, and connections to existing concrete.
  • Talk through access to your home, protection of nearby areas, and when the new surface can be used.

Businesses

Concrete work at a business affects more than the surface itself. Customers, employees, deliveries, and everyday operations all need consideration. We discuss how the property is used so the scope and schedule can account for those needs.

  • Review entrances, sidewalks, vehicle access, and the concrete areas that need construction, repair, or restoration.
  • Identify operating hours, delivery routes, and pedestrian traffic that may affect access to the work area.
  • Discuss staging options and time away from the area while concrete cures before agreeing on the work.

City & public properties

Public property projects require coordination as well as careful work. If you represent a city, public facility, or community organization, share the location and intended scope so we can discuss whether the project is a fit and what is needed to move it forward.

  • Identify the department or property representative responsible for the project and its review.
  • Bring available plans, specifications, site photos, and details about the areas that need attention.
  • Discuss public access, work area coordination, purchasing procedures, and contractor requirements for the project.

A clear plan starts with the right details.

Tell us about the property
Share the address, property type, and what you want to build or fix. Have photos or approximate dimensions ready, and let us know about any immediate access concerns.
Define the work together
Discuss the existing surface, intended use, finish preferences, and priorities. A clear scope helps establish what should be included in your project estimate.
Coordinate the next steps
Talk through site access, timing, concrete curing, and who needs to approve the work before a start date is agreed. Share any plans or requirements for the property early.
